About the company

Rooted in Karachi. Made for the work of fish farming.

Since 2004, Fine Feeds & Fisheries has made locally produced feed for fish farms and distributors. At our Deh Rehri facility, wet and dry fish move through drying, milling, and pelletizing to become floating feed and fish powder.

  • Wet and dry fish processed on site
  • Rotary drying, milling, and pelletizing
  • Feed grades for different fish growth stages
  • Jute-bag packing for farm and distributor orders

Our process

From raw material to bagged feed, every step has a purpose.

Our in-house process takes fish through a practical five-stage journey, from intake and drying to milling, pelletizing, and packing.

01

Raw material intake

Material is chipped and staged in bulk before processing begins.

02

Rotary drying

Fish is dried in a rotary drum before moving on to milling.

03

Milling & mixing

Dried material is ground and blended into a uniform base mix.

04

Pelletizing

The prepared mix is made into floating pellets or powder feed.

05

Packing & dispatch

Feed is bagged, checked, and staged for bulk or retail dispatch.
