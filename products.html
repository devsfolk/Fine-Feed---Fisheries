Feed range

Five grades & formulations, matched to every growth stage.

From fish powder feed and fine starter mash to large floating pellets — each formulation is processed from fresh wet and dry fish on dedicated lines, packed in durable jute bags.

Large floating pellets

Grow-out feed for adult stock, formulated for surface feeding so farmers can monitor intake easily.

Medium pellets

Balanced feed for growing fingerlings moving off starter mash, sized for easier feeding.

Crumble feed

Coarse crumble for juveniles transitioning from fine mash to solid pellets.

Starter mash

Fine, easily digestible powder feed for fry and the earliest feeding stages.
