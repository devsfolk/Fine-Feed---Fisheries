About us

Built around one processing line, run with care.

Fine Feeds & Fisheries is a Karachi-based fish feed manufacturer, producing high-grade aquafeed and fish powder feed for farms and distributors.

Our story

From raw material to a trusted feed brand.

Fine Feeds & Fisheries was set up to give local fish farms a dependable, locally produced alternative to imported feed. Processing both wet fish and dry fish, every batch runs through our own rotary drying, milling, and pelletizing line — producing floating pellets as well as fish powder feed.

With operations spanning beyond Deh Rehri across the region, we control quality from raw catch through to products packed in durable jute bags.

  • Rotary drum drying for stable, low-moisture feed
  • Processing both wet fish and dry fish with in-house milling & pelletizing
  • Pelleted grades and fish powder feed covering fry through to grow-out stock
  • Products packed in durable jute bags for farm and distributor dispatch

How we work

Consistency starts with the raw material.

Both wet fish and dry fish are inspected, chipped, and staged before reaching the dryer, ensuring uniform moisture reduction, milling, and fine powder formulation. That consistency carries through every pellet and powder batch, packed securely in breathable jute bags for maximum shelf life.

Packaging & Protection

Securely Packed in Jute Bags

All our feeds and fish powder are packed in tough, eco-friendly jute bags. Jute packaging allows the feed to breathe, resists tearing in transit, and preserves optimal nutritional quality from our facility to your farm.
